Transforming Care

Problem

Invasive vs Ineffective

Current treatments range from invasive deep-brain stimulation to medications with severe side effects. Patients need a non-invasive, daily-wear alternative.

Solution

Intelligent Stimulation

TUKA uses patterned vibrotactile cues to entrain motor circuits. It delivers relief exactly when tremor is detected, maximizing comfort and battery life.

Advantage

Edge Intelligence

Unlike simple vibrating bands, TUKA processes sensor data locally using on-device AI for zero-latency adaptation to individual tremor signatures.

Technology Stack

Detection & Analysis

  • High-fidelity IMU sensors for frequency tracking.
  • Real-time spectral analysis of motor intent.
  • On-chip ML models for movement classification.
  • Optional EMG integration for neuromuscular feedback.

Adaptive Therapy

  • Closed-loop modulation of stimulation intensity.
  • Phase-locked loops to counteract rhythmic tremor.
  • Low-power BLE for patient-reported outcome logging.
  • Ultra-compact medical-grade enclosure design.

The Science

Rigorous physiological monitoring meets modern signal processing.

Our roadmap includes a Hyperspectral NIRS / DCS module to measure microvascular and metabolic correlates of tremor. This allows us to calibrate stimulation based on real-time brain-to-muscle oxygenation dynamics.

hsNIRS Hemodynamic Response DCS Integration

Validation Roadmap

  1. Bench Testing: Algorithm optimization on synthetic tremor data.
  2. Pilot Study: Comfort and safety assessment with healthy volunteers.
  3. Clinical Trials: Efficacy study with ET and PD patient cohorts (n=20).
  4. Regulatory: ISO 13485 compliance and FDA/Health Canada path.
